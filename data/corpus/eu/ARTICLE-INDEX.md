## Two traps recorded here so they do not recur

**Fair use is Article 5 in the 2022 recast**, not Article 8. Article 8 is
exceptional surcharges. The numbering changed from 531/2012, and citing the old
number would score every fair-use question as a retrieval miss when retrieval
was working correctly.

**EUR-Lex mixes non-breaking spaces into references.** `32022R0612` uses
`Article\u00a05`, `32012R0531` uses `Article 5`. The converter normalises this;
if you ever parse the HTML by another route, normalise `\u00a0`, `\u202f`,
`\u2009` and `\u200b` or exact-match comparisons will silently fail.
