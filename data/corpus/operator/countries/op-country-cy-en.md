# Roaming in Cyprus

**Country:** Cyprus (CY)
**Zone:** 2 — European Union and EEA
**Regime:** capped rates, not roam like at home
**Legal basis:** EU and Western Balkans operator agreement of 1 October 2023

## Rates

| Service | Price |
|---|---|
| Data per MB | 0,90 MKD |
| Data per GB (derived) | 921,60 MKD |
| Outgoing minute | 12,90 MKD |
| Incoming minute | 3,90 MKD |
| SMS | 4,90 MKD |

## Available roaming packs

| Code | Volume | Validity | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| RM-EU-1 | 1 GB | 7 days | 299 MKD |
| RM-EU-5 | 5 GB | 30 days | 999 MKD |

## Before you travel

Activate a roaming pack before travelling. Without one, 0,90 MKD per MB means 1 GB costs roughly 921,60 MKD.

The monthly cap of 3.000 MKD applies to this destination.
Emergency calls to 112 are always free.
