# Roaming in United Arab Emirates

**Country:** United Arab Emirates (AE)
**Zone:** 3 — Rest of world
**Regime:** standard international rates
**Legal basis:** bilateral operator roaming agreements

## Rates

| Service | Price |
|---|---|
| Data per MB | 9,90 MKD |
| Data per GB (derived) | 10137,60 MKD |
| Outgoing minute | 89,90 MKD |
| Incoming minute | 39,90 MKD |
| SMS | 19,90 MKD |

## Available roaming packs

| Code | Volume | Validity | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| RM-WW-05 | 0.5 GB | 7 days | 1.499 MKD |
| RM-WW-2 | 2 GB | 30 days | 3.999 MKD |

## Before you travel

Activate a roaming pack before travelling. Without one, 9,90 MKD per MB means 1 GB costs roughly 10137,60 MKD.

The monthly cap of 3.000 MKD applies to this destination.
Emergency calls to 112 are always free.
