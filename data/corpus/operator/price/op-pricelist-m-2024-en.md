# Вардар Мобилен M
## Price list for 2024

Effective 1 January 2024. Prices in MKD including VAT.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Monthly fee | **849 MKD** |
| Domestic data | 12 GB |
| Calls to domestic networks | неограничени |
| SMS to domestic networks | неограничени |
| Maximum speed | up to 150 Mbps |

## After the allowance is used

Speed is reduced to 64 kbps for the remainder of the billing period.
No per-MB charge is applied automatically. Per-MB billing occurs only if the
subscriber explicitly enables "Continue at full speed" in My Vardar.

## Data add-ons

| Add-on | Volume | Validity | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Add-on 1 GB | 1 GB | 7 days | 169 MKD |
| Add-on 5 GB | 5 GB | 30 days | 449 MKD |
| Add-on 20 GB | 20 GB | 30 days | 999 MKD |

## Changing plan

Upgrades apply immediately with pro-rata billing. Downgrades apply from the next period,
and during the minimum term are permitted only if the new fee is at least
70% of the original.
