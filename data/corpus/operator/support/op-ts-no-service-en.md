# No service or no network

## Resolution steps

1. Toggle airplane mode. This forces re-registration and resolves the majority of cases.
2. Restart the device.
3. Check that automatic network selection is enabled. A manually locked network is a common cause after returning from abroad.
4. Remove and reinsert the SIM, checking the contacts for damage.
5. Check whether an outage is reported in your area.
6. Test the SIM in another device. If it works there, the problem is the device.

## If none of the steps help

Call 1300 or open a request in My Vardar. State the device model, the exact time the problem started, and whether it happens in one location or everywhere.
