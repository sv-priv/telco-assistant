# Change from Вардар Мобилен XL to Вардар Мобилен L
## Calculation mid-period

The change is requested on the 12th. 19 of 30 days remain.

| Item | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Credit for the old plan | 1.799 × 19 ÷ 30 | −1139,37 MKD |
| Charge for the new plan | 1.299 × 19 ÷ 30 | +822,70 MKD |
| Difference on this invoice | — | **−316,67 MKD** |
| From next month | — | 1.299 MKD |

This is a downgrade. It is allowed only if the new fee is not below 60% of the original. 1.299 against 1079,40 MKD, so the condition is met. A downgrade takes effect from the next period, not immediately.

## Data allowance after the change

The allowance is prorated over the remaining days. Traffic already used is not
refunded and does not carry over.
