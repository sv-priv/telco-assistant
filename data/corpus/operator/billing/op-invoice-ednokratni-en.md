# One-off charges

| Charge | Amount | When it applies |
|---|---|---|
| SIM replacement | 300 MKD | loss or damage |
| Paper invoice by post | 60 MKD per month | if e-invoice is not selected |
| Reconnection after suspension | 490 MKD | after the debt is settled |
| Itemised call listing | 0 MKD | on request |
| Porting the number out | 0 MKD | always free |

SIM replacement is free if it follows a technical fault confirmed in store, or when
switching to eSIM within the first 30 days of activation.
