# VAT breakdown

All published prices **include** 18% VAT. The invoice shows the amount split
out, which often looks like double charging but is not.

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Net | 761,86 MKD |
| VAT 18% | 137,14 MKD |
| **Total payable** | **899 MKD** |

The net figure is obtained by dividing the gross by 1.18, not by
subtracting 18%. That is the most common mistake when checking an invoice.

VAT-registered businesses receive an invoice with the same total; the only difference
is their ability to reclaim input tax.
