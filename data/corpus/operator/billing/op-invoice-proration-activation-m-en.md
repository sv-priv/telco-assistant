# Proration on activation
## Вардар Мобилен M

The billing period runs from the 1st to the 30th. If the service is activated on
the 18th, 13 days are charged, not a full month.

| Item | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly fee | — | 899 MKD |
| Days used | 13 of 30 | — |
| Prorated part | 899 × 13 ÷ 30 | **389,57 MKD** |

Amounts include 18% VAT.

## What else appears on the same invoice

The following full month is billed in advance on the same invoice. That is why the
first invoice usually comes to 1288,57 MKD rather than
899 MKD. This is not an error and does not repeat.

## If the service is activated on the 1st

There is no prorated part and the first invoice is 899 MKD.
