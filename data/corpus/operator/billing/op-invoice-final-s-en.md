# Final invoice after termination
## Вардар Мобилен S, terminated before the minimum term ends

The minimum term for contracts signed in 2026 is 12 months.
In this example 7 months remain.

| Item | Calculation | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Fee up to the disconnection date | prorated | per the date |
| Early termination fee | 599 × 20% × 7 | 838,60 MKD |
| Remaining device instalments | fall due immediately | per the contract |
| Unused credit | refunded | −amount |

The notice period is 15 days. The fee is charged to the end of the
notice period even if the SIM is no longer in use.

## When no fee applies

No fee applies if the minimum term has expired, if termination is within
14 days of signing, or if it follows a unilateral change of terms
by the operator that is to the customer's detriment.
