# E-03: Illegal ME
## Device: Nova X6 Pro

**Cause:** Device reported stolen

## Resolution

1. Restart the device and retry.
2. Check the SIM is seated correctly and undamaged.
3. On Nova X6 Pro: open Settings, Mobile network, and confirm automatic
   operator selection is enabled.
4. If the error repeats, reset network settings. This clears saved Wi-Fi
   passwords but does not affect your data.
5. Test the SIM in another device to determine whether the fault is with the
   SIM or with the Nova X6 Pro.

## When to contact support

If error E-03 persists after all steps, call 1300 quoting the code, the model
Nova X6 Pro, and the exact time of the last occurrence.
