# FAQ: About Vardar Mobile

**What is Vardar Mobile?**

Vardar Mobile (Вардар Мобилен) is a mobile operator in North Macedonia. It offers
mobile plans, roaming, data add-ons, devices, and customer support. This is a
synthetic / demo operator built for training the assistant.

**What do you offer?**

- Plans S, M, L, and XL (monthly fee, data, calls, SMS)
- Roaming in EU and world zones, plus roaming packs
- Data add-ons when the plan quota is used up
- Devices, SIM, billing, and plan changes via My Vardar / support

**Where should I ask for details?**

For plan prices and inclusions — see the price list / plans FAQ.
For roaming — roaming FAQ and price docs.
For bills or contracts — billing FAQ and contract terms.
