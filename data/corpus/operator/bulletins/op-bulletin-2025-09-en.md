# Network bulletin — September 2025

## Coverage expansion

During September 2025, **10 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Strumica. 5G population coverage in Strumica reached **78%**.

Work in Skopje continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.1% |
| Average 4G download | 44 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 236 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.4% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
