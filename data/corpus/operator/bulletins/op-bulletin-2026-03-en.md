# Network bulletin — March 2026

## Coverage expansion

During March 2026, **21 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Stip. 5G population coverage in Stip reached **75%**.

Work in Kocani continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.3% |
| Average 4G download | 32 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 194 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.4% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
