# Network bulletin — January 2025

## Coverage expansion

During January 2025, **8 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Skopje. 5G population coverage in Skopje reached **62%**.

Work in Tetovo continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.1% |
| Average 4G download | 28 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 180 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.2% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
