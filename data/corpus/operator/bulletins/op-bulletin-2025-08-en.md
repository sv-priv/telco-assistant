# Network bulletin — August 2025

## Coverage expansion

During August 2025, **29 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Stip. 5G population coverage in Stip reached **76%**.

Work in Kocani continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.8% |
| Average 4G download | 42 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 229 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.3% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
