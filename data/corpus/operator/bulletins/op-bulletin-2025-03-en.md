# Network bulletin — March 2025

## Coverage expansion

During March 2025, **14 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Kumanovo. 5G population coverage in Kumanovo reached **66%**.

Work in Veles continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.3% |
| Average 4G download | 32 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 194 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.4% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
