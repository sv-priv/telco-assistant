# Network bulletin — February 2025

## Coverage expansion

During February 2025, **11 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Bitola. 5G population coverage in Bitola reached **64%**.

Work in Ohrid continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.2% |
| Average 4G download | 30 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 187 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.3% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
