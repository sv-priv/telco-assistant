# Network bulletin — June 2025

## Coverage expansion

During June 2025, **23 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Ohrid. 5G population coverage in Ohrid reached **72%**.

Work in Gostivar continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.6% |
| Average 4G download | 38 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 215 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.7% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
