# Network bulletin — June 2026

## Coverage expansion

During June 2026, **8 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Kavadarci. 5G population coverage in Kavadarci reached **81%**.

Work in Kumanovo continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.6% |
| Average 4G download | 38 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 215 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.7% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
