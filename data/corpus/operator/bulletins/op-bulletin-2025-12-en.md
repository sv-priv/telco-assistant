# Network bulletin — December 2025

## Coverage expansion

During December 2025, **19 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Kocani. 5G population coverage in Kocani reached **84%**.

Work in Prilep continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.4% |
| Average 4G download | 50 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 257 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.7% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
