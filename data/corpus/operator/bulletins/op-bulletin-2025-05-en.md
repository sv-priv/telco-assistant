# Network bulletin — May 2025

## Coverage expansion

During May 2025, **20 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Tetovo. 5G population coverage in Tetovo reached **70%**.

Work in Strumica continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.5% |
| Average 4G download | 36 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 208 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.6% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
