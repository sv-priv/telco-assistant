# Network bulletin — November 2025

## Coverage expansion

During November 2025, **16 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Kavadarci. 5G population coverage in Kavadarci reached **82%**.

Work in Kumanovo continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.3% |
| Average 4G download | 48 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 250 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.6% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
