# Network bulletin — October 2025

## Coverage expansion

During October 2025, **13 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Gostivar. 5G population coverage in Gostivar reached **80%**.

Work in Bitola continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.2% |
| Average 4G download | 46 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 243 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.5% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
