# Network bulletin — July 2025

## Coverage expansion

During July 2025, **26 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Veles. 5G population coverage in Veles reached **74%**.

Work in Kavadarci continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.7% |
| Average 4G download | 40 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 222 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.2% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
