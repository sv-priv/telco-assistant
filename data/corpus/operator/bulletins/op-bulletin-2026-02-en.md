# Network bulletin — February 2026

## Coverage expansion

During February 2026, **18 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Veles. 5G population coverage in Veles reached **73%**.

Work in Kavadarci continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.2% |
| Average 4G download | 30 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 187 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.3% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
