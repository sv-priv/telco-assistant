# Network bulletin — April 2025

## Coverage expansion

During April 2025, **17 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Prilep. 5G population coverage in Prilep reached **68%**.

Work in Stip continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.4% |
| Average 4G download | 34 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 201 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.5% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
