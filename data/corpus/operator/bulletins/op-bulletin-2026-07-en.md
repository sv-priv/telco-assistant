# Network bulletin — July 2026

## Coverage expansion

During July 2026, **11 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Kocani. 5G population coverage in Kocani reached **83%**.

Work in Prilep continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.7% |
| Average 4G download | 40 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 222 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.2% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
