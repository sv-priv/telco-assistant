# Network bulletin — January 2026

## Coverage expansion

During January 2026, **15 new base stations** entered service, concentrated
around Ohrid. 5G population coverage in Ohrid reached **71%**.

Work in Gostivar continues, with completion expected next quarter.

## Service quality

| Indicator | Value |
|---|---|
| Network availability | 99.1% |
| Average 4G download | 28 Mbps |
| Average 5G download | 180 Mbps |
| Call setup success rate | 98.2% |

These are network-wide averages and do not represent a guaranteed speed at any single
location.
