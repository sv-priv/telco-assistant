# Roaming zones, regimes and rates — 2026

North Macedonia is not a member of the European Union or the European Economic Area.
Regulation (EU) 2022/612, which establishes Roam Like At Home, therefore does not
apply directly to our subscribers.

Travelling to Greece is not the same as travelling to Serbia, even though both are
in Europe.

| Zone | Regime | Data | Outgoing minute |
|---|---|---|---|
| Western Balkans | roam like at home, no surcharge | 0 MKD/MB | 0 MKD/min |
| European Union and EEA | capped rates, not roam like at home | 0,90 MKD/MB | 12,90 MKD/min |
| Rest of world | standard international rates | 9,90 MKD/MB | 89,90 MKD/min |

## Zone 1 — Western Balkans
Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, Montenegro, Serbia. Basis: Western Balkans Regional Roaming Agreement, in force since 1 July 2021. Domestic allowance applies with no surcharge.

## Zone 2 — EU and EEA
the 27 EU member states, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway. Basis: EU and Western Balkans operator agreement of 1 October 2023. This is not Roam Like At Home.

## Zone 3 — Rest of world
all other countries, including Turkey, Switzerland, the United Kingdom and the USA. Basis: bilateral operator roaming agreements.

**Note.** Turkey and Switzerland are frequently assumed to fall under the European
regime. Neither is an EU or EEA member, and neither is party to the Western Balkans
Regional Roaming Agreement. Both are billed at Zone 3 rates.
