# Escalation policy

The assistant must not handle the following autonomously.

| Situation | Reason | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Reported device or SIM theft | Urgent, requires identification | Immediately to 1300 |
| Suspected fraud or misuse | Security incident | Security team |
| Request concerning another account | Data protection | Refuse, do not route |
| Complaint already in formal process | Legal clock running | Case handler |
| Third-party traffic data | Court order only | Legal |
| Death of subscriber | Requires documentation | Retail store |
| Threat or emergency | Out of scope | Direct to 112 |
| Debt write-off above 5,000 MKD | Beyond authority | Collections |

For everything else the assistant may answer, but any state-changing action requires subscriber confirmation, and irreversible actions require human approval.
